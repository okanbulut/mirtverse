### Teaching and learning

- [Teaching labs for measurement
  courses](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/teaching-labs.md):

  Interactive learnr labs in which students fit live mirt models and
  make the decisions a psychometrician makes, from scoring and model
  choice to interpretation and fairness. Includes an instructor guide
  and answer key.

- [A course map for
  instructors](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/course-map.md):

  A suggested 12-week syllabus for a university course in item response
  theory, linking each week to mirtverse articles, labs, functions and
  datasets.

- [The instructor
  toolkit](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/instructor-toolkit.md):

  Build interactive labs on your own data, adapt the built-in labs and
  the book’s chapters, add exercises and questions, swap datasets, and
  check everything before class.

- [An AI assistant for your course
  materials](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/instructor-mcp.md):

  Connect Claude or an OpenAI assistant to your mirtverse course folder
  and revise labs, chapters and notes, or bring your existing materials
  into mirtverse, by describing what you want.

- [Glossary of IRT
  terms](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/glossary.md):

  Plain-language definitions of the terms used in item response theory,
  each with the mirtverse or mirt function that computes it, and
  pictures where a picture helps.

### AI in measurement

Artificial intelligence and machine learning for psychometricians. These
articles are also in the AI Hub menu.

- [AI Hub: artificial intelligence for
  psychometricians](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/ai-hub.md):

  Start here to learn how artificial intelligence and machine learning
  fit into psychometrics and educational measurement: a learning path,
  what mirtverse can already do with AI, and where to read more.

- [Machine learning for psychometricians: a translation
  table](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/ai-translation.md):

  More than 40 machine learning and AI terms, each with what it means,
  the psychometric idea closest to it, and where the two part ways.

- [AI in measurement: a reading
  list](https://www.okanbulut.com/mirtverse/articles/ai-reading.md):

  An annotated list of books, papers and guidelines on artificial
  intelligence in psychometrics and educational measurement, grouped by
  topic, for psychometricians who are new to the area.
